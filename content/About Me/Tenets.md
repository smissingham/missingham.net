# Core Principles
> Knowledge is the greatest wealth, and just like money we grow our knowledge bank best when we share.
> 
> If I can condense and hour of my research time into 5 minutes of your learning time, and you can do the same for me, then together we can be greater than the sum of our parts.

> Resources must be publicly available and openly permissive.
>
> I want all of my material to be accessible to anybody, regardless of their background or their finances.
> While I welcome and appreciated financial support, I will always endeavour to release my own content and creations with open permissive license.
> 
> If content is locked behind pay walls, then newcomers to the industry with limited finances cannot grow like I have been able to.

> Compliance, trust and discretion are critical to a healthy career.
> 
> I will NEVER share publicly any information that belongs to my paying customers, employers etc. and will never allow my affiliates to do so.
> 
> I have the privilege of working for the largest companies in the world, with data, code and other information that is subject to compliance and privacy requirements. Such resources always remain locked tight on encrypted secure devices and will never be exposed in the public domain.

> Privacy is a right that must be protected with vigilance.
> 
> Many tools in the technology field collect telemetry for their own betterment. While I respect software that uses telemetry for improving service, given the sensitivity of the information I work on I am always on the lookout for ways to prevent or disable telemetry collection from my devices.

> Respect is a right, not a privilege.
> 
> I will never stand for bullying or discrimination, in the real world or the digital one.
> 
> Licenses are a part of that respect. I will always do my best to adhere to licenses of any content or software I use or present. It is only fair that I uphold this practice myself if I expect others to do so of my work.
> 
> Attributions should always be made, even if the license doesn't require it. Wherever possible, I aim to attribute thanks to those who came before me and built content or tools upon which I've built my career.

> Integrity is the sibling of trust and respect.
> 
> I wear my heart on my sleeve, and surround myself with those who do the same.
> 
>  My colleagues, friends, and customers will always know the person they're speaking with. I will never willingly sell or advocate for something based on obscured truths or agendas. 

> We are all human, none of us perfect. 
> 
> Despite this lengthy list of principles I aim to uphold in myself and those around me, we must forgive each other our transgressions. 
> 
> The best way to uphold these principles when they're mistaken or forgotten is through friendly constructive feedback. I ask that people do this for me where I may have offended, as I will do for you.
